Our Story

Redefining Luxury Beauty in Varanasi

True Aura Family Salon is Varanasi's most premium luxury salon — and the largest family salon in all of Uttar Pradesh. Located on Shivpur Coat Bypass Road, Varanasi, we are easily accessible from every part of the city.

Spread across 2,200 sq ft, we offer world-class hair, skin, nail, and bridal beauty services for the entire family. From advanced keratin treatments and luxury facials to celebrity-style bridal makeovers — every service is delivered by expert professionals using premium international brands.
